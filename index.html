Tailored for Civil Services & Legal Study

Explore robust, immersive features designed to make constitutional study visual, interactive, and seamless.

Bilingual & Side-by-Side

Read the Constitution of India in English and Hindi. Instantly switch languages or view them side-by-side with fluid interface transitions.

106+ Amendments Timeline

Trace constitutional evolution via an interactive timeline. Review side-by-side "Before vs. After" comparative breakdowns of changes.

Preamble & Interactive Glossary

Dive deep into the Preamble. Tap on key concepts like Sovereign or Secular to view exam-targeted historical and legal definitions.

Fast Offline Global Search

Search concurrently across Articles, Amendments, Schedules, and Cases. Powered by Isar local indexing for ultra-low latency results.

Landmark Cases Explorer

Learn historical Supreme Court cases (e.g., Kesavananda Bharati). Study court findings, judge bench sizes, and core legal precedents.

UPSC Exam Study Aids

Benefit from exam trap warnings, memory mnemonics, cross-linked article tags, and exam-importance ratings to boost preparation.
