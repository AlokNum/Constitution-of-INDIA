What's Inside

Built for Serious Study

Robust, immersive features designed to make constitutional study visual, interactive, and seamless — online or offline.

Bilingual & Side-by-Side

Read the Constitution in English and Hindi simultaneously. Instantly switch languages or view them side-by-side with smooth transitions.

106+ Amendments Timeline

Trace constitutional evolution via an interactive timeline. Compare "Before vs. After" breakdowns of every amendment.

Preamble & Glossary

Tap on key concepts like Sovereign or Secular to open exam-targeted historical and legal definitions right in context.

Fast Offline Search

Search concurrently across Articles, Amendments, Schedules, and Cases — powered by Isar local indexing for ultra-low latency.

Landmark Cases Explorer

Study Supreme Court milestones like Kesavananda Bharati — court findings, bench sizes, and lasting legal precedents.

UPSC Study Aids

Exam trap warnings, memory mnemonics, cross-linked article tags, and importance ratings to sharpen your preparation.
