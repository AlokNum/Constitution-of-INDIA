Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Samvidhan (संविधान) mobile application (the "App") handles user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a positive experience.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Offline-First Design: The App is designed as an offline-first tool. Your study data — including bookmarked articles, search queries, notes, and progress metrics — are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

Personal Information: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or location data.

2. Third-Party Analytics and Crash Reporting

To improve performance, reliability, and usability, we may use trusted third-party services — specifically Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics). These services may collect:

  • Device type, operating system version, and anonymous device identifiers.
  • App launch times, session duration, and feature interaction events (e.g., toggling English/Hindi, searching, opening details).
  • Crash logs and diagnostic reports in the event of an unexpected crash.

This information is purely technical and cannot be used to personally identify you. We use it solely to debug issues and optimise future app updates. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more on how Firebase handles data.

3. Local Device Permissions

Depending on how you use the App, it may request certain device permissions:

  • Network Access: Required to fetch over-the-air database updates (syncing new judgments or minor corrections) and for anonymous crash reporting.
  • Storage Access: Used solely to store the local database file containing Constitution text and your bookmarks.

4. Data Retention

Because your bookmarks and study preferences are stored locally on your device, they are retained until you delete the App or clear its storage cache through your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is educational and suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us immediately.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated policy on this page and revising the "Last Updated" date. We recommend checking this page periodically.
