Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Samvidhan (संविधान) mobile application (the "App"), developed and maintained by Alok Kumar ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you use our App. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data Safety Summary

This section provides a plain-language summary of our data practices as required by app store data safety declarations:

Category Details
Data collected Device type, OS version, anonymous device identifiers, app interactions, crash logs
How it is collected Automatically, via the Firebase SDK (Google Analytics for Firebase & Firebase Crashlytics)
Why it is collected To analyse app usage, fix bugs, and improve user experience
Data shared with third parties Only with Google (Firebase), as described below. No data is sold to any third party.
PII collected None. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data.

1. Information We Collect

1a. Information You Provide

The App does not require you to create an account or submit any personal information to use its core features. Your bookmarks, notes, and study preferences are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to our servers.

1b. Information Collected Automatically via Firebase

We have integrated Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics) into the App. These services automatically collect the following technical and usage data:

  • Device information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, and screen resolution.
  • Anonymous identifiers: A randomly generated, resettable device identifier (not linked to your identity).
  • Usage events: App launch and close times, session duration, features accessed (e.g., article search, English/Hindi toggle, bookmarking), and navigation patterns.
  • Crash & diagnostic reports: Stack traces, app state at the time of a crash, and device conditions that may have contributed to an error.

None of the above data can be used to personally identify you.

2. How We Use Your Information

The technical data collected via Firebase is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Analysing aggregate app usage to understand which features are most valuable.
  • Identifying and fixing crashes, bugs, and performance issues.
  • Improving the overall user experience in future updates.

We do not use this data for targeted advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond improving the App.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your data with any outside parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

Data is shared only in the following limited circumstance:

  • Google Firebase (service provider): Anonymous usage and crash data is processed by Google LLC under their role as a data processor. This data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's data processing terms.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law or a valid court order.

4. Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions on your device:

  • Internet / Network access: Required to send anonymous crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics and to check for over-the-air content updates.
  • Storage access: Used solely to store the local SQLite database containing Constitution text, your bookmarks, and offline content.

No permission is used to access your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or any other sensitive device feature.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information processed through our App. Analytics and crash data transmitted to Firebase is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS. Your locally stored data (bookmarks and preferences) never leaves your device unless you explicitly back it up through your device's own backup system.

No personally identifiable information is stored on our servers because we do not collect any.

6. Data Retention

  • Local data (bookmarks, notes, progress): Retained on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its storage via your device settings.
  • Firebase Analytics data: Retained for up to 14 months by Google per their standard retention policy, after which it is automatically deleted.
  • Crashlytics data: Retained for 90 days by Google.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is educational in nature and suitable for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). The only data automatically collected is anonymous technical data via Firebase, which is not linked to any individual.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns, please contact us at the email below.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You may limit Firebase Analytics data collection at any time by enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). This resets your advertising ID and signals to Firebase that you prefer not to be tracked for analytics.

Because we do not hold any personally identifiable data ourselves, there is no user account to delete. Your local App data can be erased by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the App after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
