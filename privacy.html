Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Samvidhan (संविधान) mobile application (the "App") handles user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience while studying the Constitution of India.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Offline-First Design: The App is designed as an offline-first tool. This means that your study data, including bookmarked articles, search queries, notes, and progress metrics, are saved locally on your device using a secure local database (Isar). We do not collect or store this data on external servers.

Personal Information: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or location data. You can use all core features of the App without creating an account or providing any personal details.

2. Third-Party Analytics and Crash Reporting

To improve the performance, reliability, and usability of the App, we may use trusted third-party services, specifically Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics). These services collect anonymous diagnostics and usage statistics, which may include:

  • Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (anonymous).
  • App launch times, session duration, and feature interaction events (e.g., toggling English/Hindi, searching, opening details).
  • Crash logs and diagnostic reports in the event of an unexpected app crash.

This information is purely technical and cannot be used to identify you personally. We use this data solely to debug issues and optimize future app updates.

3. Local Device Permissions

Depending on how you use the App, it may request certain permissions on your device, such as:

  • Network Access: Required to fetch over-the-air database updates (syncing new judgments or minor corrections) and for anonymous crash reporting.
  • Storage Access: Used solely to store the local database file containing the Constitution text and your bookmarks.

4. Data Retention

Because your bookmarks and study preferences are stored locally on your device, they are retained until you delete the App or clear the App's storage cache through your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is educational and suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will take immediate action to remove it.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top. We recommend checking this page periodically for any updates.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at:

support@samvidhanapp.in
